Talk to it and it talks back. It remembers what you tell it, learns how you like to be addressed, and answers to whatever name you give it — yours doesn't have to be called Saira.

Name it yourself

Call it Jarvis, Nova, Krishna — anything you like. It listens for your name phonetically, so it still wakes up even when speech recognition spells it differently.

It remembers

Past conversations are stored encrypted and recalled when relevant. Say “call me Sai” once and it sticks, on every reply from then on.

Runs locally

Speech recognition, the language model and the voice all run on our own server. No third-party AI service sees a word of your conversation.

Speaks Telugu too

Ask in Telugu and it answers in Telugu. Ask in English and it stays in English, even when it remembers an older conversation in another language.

Interrupt it

Say “stop” while it's talking and it stops mid-sentence, the way a person would. No waiting for it to finish.
